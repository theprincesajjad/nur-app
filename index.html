nur.

one hadith a day, drawn by hand.

privacy

nur. collects no data. there are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. nothing you do in the app leaves your phone.

the pictures you draw, the colours you pick and the days you open the app are saved only on your device, so the app and its widgets can show them. deleting the app deletes them.

purchases (the supporter unlock and tips) are handled entirely by Apple or Google. nur. never sees your payment details.

when you share a card, it goes only where you send it.
